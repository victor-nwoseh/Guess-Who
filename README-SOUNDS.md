# Sound Effects — File Requirements

Place all files in `client/public/sounds/`. All files must be **MP3 format**.

## Required Sound Files

| Filename | Description | Duration | Suggested Style |
|----------|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| `card-flip.mp3` | Short snap/flip sound when a character card is eliminated or restored | < 1 sec | Paper flip, card snap, or soft "whoosh" |
| `button-tap.mp3` | Subtle tap/click for button presses | < 0.5 sec | Soft UI click, gentle pop |
| `victory.mp3` | Celebratory sound when you win a round | 2-3 sec | Short fanfare, chime cascade, or triumphant jingle |
| `defeat.mp3` | Subdued sound when you lose a round | 2-3 sec | Descending tones, gentle "wah-wah", or soft buzzer |
| `turn-start.mp3` | Notification that it's now your turn | < 1 sec | Gentle chime, soft ping, or notification ding |
| `snipe-attempt.mp3` | Dramatic sound when a player initiates a guess (snipe) | 1-2 sec | Suspenseful hit, dramatic whoosh, or tension sting |
| `question-received.mp3` | Alert that the opponent has asked you a question | < 1 sec | Message notification, soft bell, or pop |
| `player-joined.mp3` | Sound when a second player joins the lobby | < 1 sec | Welcome chime, join sound, or upbeat ping |
| `match-found.mp3` | Sound when Quick Match pairs you with an opponent | 1-2 sec | Matchmaking success, connection sound, or double chime |

## Free Sources

- **Pixabay Sound Effects** — pixabay.com/sound-effects (free, no attribution required)
- **Mixkit** — mixkit.co/free-sound-effects (free, no attribution required)
- **Freesound** — freesound.org (free, check individual licenses)

## Notes

- Keep all files small (under 100KB each ideally) for fast loading
- Sounds should feel modern and clean — not retro or overly gamey
- Victory/defeat sounds should be distinct but not jarring
- All short sounds (< 1 sec) should have no silence padding at start/end
